Your camera, rebuilt while you talk.

Stream Live turns your webcam into a live AI presenter and your microphone into a clean cloned voice, then hands both to OBS, Zoom, Teams, Chrome or WhatsApp as if they were a camera and a mic. Nothing to render, nothing to upload: what you say is what goes out.

What you get

Two devices other apps believe

Once you are running, your app list gains a camera and a microphone that did not exist before. Point anything at them — a stream, a meeting, a browser call.

An AI picture, as a camera

On Windows the installer adds Stream Live Camera and any app can select it: OBS, Zoom, Teams, Google Meet, WhatsApp Web. On a Mac the picture goes out through OBS instead — one free program, three clicks, covered under Mac install.

A cloned voice, as a microphone

Voice on its own, or paired with video. Noise cancellation is on by default and clears the room out of your mic. On a Mac a free silent speaker called BlackHole carries it to your call app.

Landscape or tall

Full frame for OBS scenes, or an exact 9:16 portrait window for phone-shaped video — calls and captures both receive it tall.

Windows and macOS

Windows 10 and 11 get both devices straight from the installer, nothing else to fetch. A Mac installs the same way and then needs the two free helpers, because Apple only lets an app it has approved host a system webcam.

How it works

Three moves, then you are live

  1. Install and sign up. Run the installer, open Stream Live, create an account in the app. It works the moment it is created.
  2. Pick a look and a voice. Choose a style, then a face or voice reference. Video, voice, or both together.
  3. Select it in your app. In the call or streaming app, choose Stream Live Camera and Stream Live Microphone — on Windows the audio one may be listed as CABLE Input or VB-Audio Virtual Cable, which is the same device — on a Mac they are named OBS Virtual Camera and BlackHole 2ch. Then press Start in Stream Live. Your own camera stays off-air.
On a Mac? The short version of this page is the Mac install walk-through: approve the app once, allow camera and microphone, add the two free helpers, then point OBS at the Stream Live window.

Mac install

Getting Stream Live onto a Mac

Five steps, about three minutes. Do them in order; steps 1 and 2 are one-time, and steps 4 and 5 are what make a call app see the AI picture and hear the AI voice.

  1. Move the app into Applications. Double-click the .dmg you downloaded, drag Stream Live onto Applications, then eject the disk. Keep it in Applications — running it from the Downloads folder is what makes later updates fail.
  2. Clear the “damaged” warning, once. Stream Live is not signed with a paid Apple developer certificate, so the first launch can say “Stream Live is damaged and can’t be opened”. That is the missing signature talking, not a broken download — the file you got is complete. Two things to do: finish step 1 so the app really is in Applications, then open Terminal (Cmd + Space, type Terminal, press Return) and paste this line, then press Return:
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Stream Live.app"
    Launch Stream Live normally after that — it stays cleared and you never do this again. If Terminal answers No such file, the app is not in Applications yet, so go back to step 1. If it asks for permission, run the same line with sudo in front of it and type your Mac login password — nothing appears on screen while you type it, which is normal.
  3. Allow the camera and the microphone. In System Settings → Privacy & Security, switch Stream Live on under both Camera and Microphone. Without them the preview stays black and no voice is cloned.
  4. Install the two free helpers. They do the job the Windows installer does with bundled drivers: one carries sound, one carries video.
    • BlackHole 2ch — a silent virtual speaker; the AI voice goes in here. Clicking it downloads the installer straight into your Downloads folder. It is free software and that link asks you for nothing — no sign-up, no email, no payment page. After installing it, log out and back in, and it appears under System Settings → Sound and in the app’s “Audio out” list. If the link ever fails, message us on Telegram and we will send you the file.
    • OBS Studio — turns the Stream Live window into a webcam.
  5. Put the AI feed into your call. On the stream screen, open the OBS window and turn Stream Live Microphone on, so the cloned voice moves to BlackHole 2ch. Then:
    1. In OBS: Sources → + → Window Capture → pick “Stream Live OBS”.
    2. In OBS: Controls → Start Virtual Camera.
    3. In Zoom, Teams, Meet or WhatsApp: Camera → OBS Virtual Camera, Microphone → BlackHole 2ch.
Want to be heard next to the AI voice? Open Audio MIDI Setup, create a Multi-Output Device that combines your own microphone with BlackHole 2ch, and pick that as the call app’s input. OBS carries picture only, which is why the call app takes its microphone from BlackHole rather than from OBS.

macOS only accepts a system webcam from software Apple has approved with a special entitlement, so on a Mac the picture always travels through OBS. Everything else — the styles, portrait mode, noise cancellation, your minute balance — works the same as on Windows.

Minutes

Pay for time, not for seats

Stream Live is metered in minutes of airtime. Your balance sits in the app and counts down only while you are actually on air, so a session you never started costs you nothing.

  • Packages and prices are listed in the app under Plans, and your remaining time under Minutes.
  • Pay for a package, then send the reference from the same screen — the app shows the payment and contact details to use.
  • An administrator adds the minutes to your account, and the app shows the new balance without you restarting it.
  • Time does not expire, and one account can be used on more than one computer.

Sign-in trouble

Locked out

If you reached this page from an email link, the link reached us — but Stream Live does not change a password through a web form, so there is nothing to fill in here.

  • Open the app and use Plans to send your account email to the contact shown there.
  • An administrator sets a new password and sends it back to you.
  • If the app will not start, message on Telegram or reply to the email you signed up with — both reach the team.
